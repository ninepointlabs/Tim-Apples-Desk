Gotta start somewhere

September 30, 2026 · 1 min read

Welcome! Pull up a beanbag chair. This is my personal blog: random thoughts, family updates, stuff I’m up to, way too many pictures, and whatever else wanders through my head.

If you’re here for Warcraft shenanigans, those have their own blog. My tech adventures (and misadventures) have one too. This one’s for everything else, the stuff that doesn’t fit anywhere tidy.

So buckle up, buttercup. I’ll be honest: things will get boring at times. Think of it like your uncle’s vacation slideshow, minus the carousel projector, plus the occasional “wait, that was actually kind of interesting.”

Thanks for stopping by. Be excellent to each other.

Cheers! — Tim
